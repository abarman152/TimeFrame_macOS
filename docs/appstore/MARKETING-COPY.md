## Description  (max 4000)

```
Time Frame is a native focus timer built around the Pomodoro method — designed
from the ground up for Apple platforms, not ported from somewhere else.

Set up a work/break rhythm that fits how you actually focus, save it, and start
it in one tap. Time Frame keeps the count for you and gets out of the way.

FOCUS THAT FITS YOU
• Configurable focus, short-break, and long-break intervals
• Save timers you reuse — Deep Work, Study, Admin — and start them instantly
• Task templates that carry their own timing
• A session planner for stringing intervals into a full work block

SEE WHERE YOUR TIME GOES
• A statistics dashboard: focus totals, streaks, and per-timer breakdowns
• A Today view for an at-a-glance sense of the day
• History you own — everything stays on your device

EVERYWHERE YOU WORK
• Mac menu bar timer with live countdown and controls
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Control Center controls to start, pause, and skip
• A Live Activity that keeps the countdown on your Lock Screen (iPhone)
• Siri Shortcuts and App Intents to start a timer by voice or automation
• Calendar events for your focus sessions (Mac)
• Gentle notifications at the moments that matter — never a per-second buzz

BUILT RIGHT
• Native SwiftUI with the Liquid Glass look
• Your data lives on your device — no account, no tracking, no ads
• Designed for accessibility: VoiceOver labels and system text sizing

Time Frame does one thing: it helps you start, and keeps honest time while you
focus. That's the whole point.
```
